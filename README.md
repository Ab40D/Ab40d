## / PROFILE

Electronics engineer focused on **systems integration, building automation, industrial control, and IoT**.

I work at the intersection of field devices, controllers, communication protocols, automation logic, and human-facing software. My goal is to make heterogeneous systems work together as one coherent environment — from the physical layer to the interface.

I am especially interested in **local-first architectures**, interoperability, reliable control, and practical engineering that can be tested beyond a screen.
