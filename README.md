## `01` — Beyond a Single Discipline

I'm Abdelkhalek Mammeri, an Electronics and Embedded Systems student, engineer-minded developer, and technology builder from Algeria.

My interests extend beyond traditional software development. I enjoy exploring how electronics, software, communication protocols, automation, and artificial intelligence can work together to create intelligent, interconnected systems.

I work across both physical and digital environments — from embedded devices and hardware interfaces to APIs, software platforms, automation workflows, and interactive 3D experiences.

**My approach is simple: understand the system, connect its components, and build something meaningful.**

## `03` — What I'm Building

### SE7ENX — Intelligent Building Ecosystem

An ongoing engineering initiative exploring a new way to interact with and manage intelligent environments.

The vision is to bridge physical building infrastructure with immersive digital interfaces.

- KNX and smart building integration
- IoT connectivity and MQTT communication
- Real-time device monitoring and control
- 3D digital twins and spatial interfaces
- Voice and gesture-based interaction
- AI-assisted building experiences
- Local-first architecture and system interoperability

**Vision:** Make buildings more interactive, understandable, and intelligent.

### Spatial Computing & Human–Machine Interaction

Exploring new interaction paradigms between humans and connected environments.

Working with computer vision, hand tracking, MediaPipe, Three.js, and real hardware integration to experiment with interfaces that go beyond traditional screens and control panels.

### Embedded & IoT Experiments

Developing practical prototypes that connect electronic hardware to software applications, exploring microcontrollers, wireless communication, automation, and device control.

### Industrial Automation & BMS

Exploring building and industrial control systems through KNX, PLC technologies, SCADA concepts, BACnet, and engineering simulation environments.
