## `01` — WHO I AM

I'm Abdelkhalek Mammeri, an Electronics and Embedded Systems student, multidisciplinary technology builder, and Full-Stack IoT Developer from Algeria.

I work across the boundaries of electronics, software engineering, industrial automation, connected devices, and artificial intelligence.

My approach is to understand how different technologies interact, integrate them into coherent architectures, and turn engineering concepts into functional systems.

I am particularly interested in bridging the physical and digital worlds through intelligent infrastructure, automation, and human-machine interaction.

> I don't just want to build software that interacts with hardware. I want to engineer systems where hardware, software, and intelligence work as one.
